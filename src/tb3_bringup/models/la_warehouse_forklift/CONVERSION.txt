The GLB was imported with Assimp 5.2 using Triangulate, PreTransformVertices and GenSmoothNormals, then exported as OBJ. Embedded JPEG images Forklift_Albedo and Forklift_Normal were copied unchanged from GLB buffer views to materials/textures. Material diffuse/normal paths were rewritten to those local images. Geometry bounds before the SDF scale: x[-0.567626,0.567626], y[-0.874059,2.29449], z[-0.0157103,2.13023] metres. The original primitive collision shapes were retained and uniformly scaled.
